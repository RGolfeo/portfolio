About

Hi, I'm Ryan — support instincts, growth execution.

I've spent 7+ years on both sides of the customer relationship — first as a support specialist helping people solve problems, and now as a growth marketer helping businesses find new customers. That combination is my edge: I understand what makes a prospect say yes, and what makes a customer stay.

I started in high-volume BPO support at Verizon, Sutherland, and TaskUs, where I learned to handle pressure, troubleshoot fast, and keep people satisfied even on a bad day. From there I moved into growth marketing and lead generation — running outreach campaigns, managing CRM pipelines, and booking appointments for sales teams.

Today I work remotely with US-based businesses, bringing both the outbound hustle of growth marketing and the calm, detail-oriented instincts of customer support to every project.
