Featured case study · Healthcare credentialing

A lead-generation system built for visibility, not guesswork.

For a healthcare credentialing business, I supported business development by researching qualified practices, organizing outreach-ready records, and building a consistent process from prospecting through follow-up.

0+prospects tracked at the captured stage
0high-priority opportunities identified
0U.S. markets represented in the dashboard
7-stepresearch → verify → score → personalize → outreach → log → follow up
01
Problem

Prospecting needed structure before it could scale.

The business needed a process that kept qualified prospects, outreach status, and next actions visible instead of scattered across notes and tabs.

02
Strategy

Define who is worth contacting—and why.

I researched healthcare providers and practices using public sources, reviewed specialty and location signals, and documented useful context for more personalized outreach.

03
Execution

Turn research into an operational pipeline.

Prospects were organized by segment, specialty, market, lead source, priority, status, and next action. Outreach and follow-up were supported by repeatable messaging and daily reporting.

04
Outcome

Cleaner priorities and a pipeline that could be reviewed quickly.

The captured dashboard showed 90+ structured prospects and 17 high-priority opportunities—without claiming downstream revenue or closed clients that were outside my control.
